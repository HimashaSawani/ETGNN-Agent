# Evidence Grounding & Epistemic Anti-Hallucination Policy

## 1. Core Principles of Grounding

In financial intelligence and forensic graph analysis, automated AI claims can have serious real-world legal and regulatory repercussions. The `ETGNN-Agent` implements an uncompromising evidence-grounding policy based on three foundational tenets:

1. **Strict Citation Discipline**: Every empirical claim must be tethered to an immutable `EvidenceRecord` possessing a verified SHA-256 provenance hash.
2. **Epistemic Humility**: Models produce statistical risk scores under distribution shifts, not definitive judicial proof of criminality.
3. **Zero Semantic Hallucination**: Feature columns in the Elliptic benchmark are anonymized local (1–93) and aggregated (94–165) statistics. Speculating on human-identifiable attributes (e.g. "wallet owner name", "exchange account", "IP address") is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Prohibited Epistemic Phrases

The validator scans all narrative text, findings, and summaries for prohibited certainty claims. Detection results in immediate claim degradation or report regeneration.

| Prohibited Category | Prohibited Phrases | Required Epistemic Replacement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definitive Guilt** | `"guaranteed illicit"`, `"proven criminal"`, `"proves guilt"`, `"criminal certainty"` | `"high model-predicted illicit risk probability"` |
| **Definitive Innocence**| `"guaranteed licit"`, `"definitely clean"`, `"100% legitimate"` | `"low risk score under historical baseline"` |
| **Causal Attribution** | `"this edge proves laundering"`, `"attributions confirm fraud routing"` | `"edge sensitivity identifies model representation shifts under perturbation"` |
| **Fabricated Identity**| `"wallet belonging to Russian exchange"`, `"Silk Road operator balance"` | `"anonymized feature vector (feat_0..feat_164)"` |

---

## 3. Claim Classification Taxonomy

The `ClaimValidator` categorizes all findings into one of five formal epistemic tiers:

- **`SUPPORTED`**: The finding is directly verifiable against an existing evidence record within numerical tolerance ($\le 5\%$), references a valid evidence ID, and avoids prohibited phrasing.
- **`DERIVED`**: The finding is logically inferred from multiple evidence records (e.g. probability spread computation between models).
- **`HYPOTHESIS`**: An analytical conjecture framed with appropriate uncertainty language.
- **`UNVERIFIED`**: An assertion lacking direct evidence or referencing an unverified ID. Automatically demoted to `uncertain_findings`.
- **`UNSUPPORTED`**: A claim contradicting source evidence, exceeding numerical tolerances, or containing hallucinated attributes. Replaced or deleted during validation.
