## 1. Provenance Requirements & Scientific Standards

In crypto-asset forensics, an assertion without provenance is legally and scientifically inadmissible. The **ETGNN-Agent** framework mandates that:
1. Every piece of structured or unstructured data presented to human analysts or downstream reasoning models must possess a deterministic `evidence_id`.
2. Every evidence record must cite its exact source artifact, file path, and cryptographic checksum.
3. When research claims or historical reports disagree, the system must **explicitly surface the discrepancy** rather than silently selecting or merging one value.

## 4. Conflict Resolution & Disclosure Policy

When a discrepancy exists between an authoritative source and an earlier exploratory report:
1. **Rule of Machine Ground Truth**: The value directly produced by deterministic inference on the verified checkpoint takes precedence.
2. **Explicit Variance Disclosure**: Rather than silently overwriting, the tool returns a `has_historical_conflict = True` flag with an explanation string.
   - *Example (XGBoost AUPRC)*:
     - Verified Benchmark: `0.547215` (from `xgboost.json` inference).
     - Draft Thesis Log: `0.5982` (from exploratory hyperparameter run).
     - System Action: Cites `0.547215` as authoritative; appends conflict note: `"Authoritative checkpoint xgboost.json reproduces AUPRC=0.547215. An earlier thesis exploratory run recorded AUPRC=0.5982 using alternative depth parameters."`
